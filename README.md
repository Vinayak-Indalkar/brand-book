# Quantira Technologies — Brand Guidelines (v1.0)

Welcome to the **Quantira Technologies Official Brand Guidelines & Design System**.

---

## 🏢 Company Overview
- **Company**: Quantira Technologies (Version 1.0 Brand Guidelines)
- **Positioning**: A Hyderabad-based software solutions company creating agile, future-ready digital ecosystems and resilient IT infrastructures.
- **Headquarters**: Hyderabad, India

---

## 📐 Logo & Identity System
- **Origin & Symbolism**: Combines the **Phi Symbol ($\varnothing$)**, a **Planet with Orbit**, and **Quantum Circuits** into a stylized letter **"Q"**.
- **Horizontal Lockup**: Logo Mark on the left + uppercase wordmark **"QUANTIRA TECHNOLOGIES"** on the right.
- **Variants**:
  - **Full Color Gradient**: `#6cbae7` to `#2a5ba9` on Pure White.
  - **Dark Background Format**: Luminous gradient mark with white wordmark on Core Black.
  - **Outline Version**: Stroke-only vector geometry.
  - **Mono-Color**: Single-color Core Black (`#020202`) for high-contrast print.
- **Clear Space / Safe Size**: Margin defined by **$X$** on all four sides around the logo bounding box.

---

## 🎨 Color System & Distribution (50-30-20 Rule)

| Role | Color Name | Hex / Values | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Dominant Canvas (50%)** | Pure White | `#ffffff`<br>RGB: `(255, 255, 255)`<br>CMYK: `(0, 0, 0, 0)` | Primary background providing breathing room. |
| **Structure / Text (30%)** | Core Black | `#020202`<br>RGB: `(2, 2, 2)`<br>CMYK: `(0, 0, 0, 99)` | Typography, high-contrast layouts, and structure. |
| **Accent / CTA (20%)** | Blue Gradient | `#6cbae7` to `#2a5ba9`<br>`linear-gradient(135deg, #6cbae7 0%, #2a5ba9 100%)` | Interactive elements, CTAs, and key brand moments. |

---

## ✍️ Typography Hierarchy
- **Primary Font**: **Lexend** (Sans-serif, used for Titles, Headings, and Lead copy to enhance readability).
- **Secondary Font**: **Noto Sans** (Used for Body paragraphs, UI buttons, and functional links).
- **Monospace Font**: **JetBrains Mono** (Used for telemetry, code, and tokens).

## 🌐 Visual Assets & Patterns
- **Geometric Pattern**: Low-contrast isometric cube / blockchain quantum lattice texture.
- **Imagery**: Tech-focused photography, AI hardware/chips, server infrastructure, abstract quantum graphics, and modern workspace environments.
- **Applications**: Corporate signage, executive stationery, NFC business cards, merchandise (tote bags, tech mugs), and social media templates.
