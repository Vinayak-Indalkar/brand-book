02 / Logo System

Origin & Identity Symbolism

The Quantira Technologies logo mark combines three foundational concepts into a unified, stylized letter "Q":

01. Phi Symbol ($\varnothing$)

Mathematical balance, golden ratio, and structural harmony in system engineering.

02. Planetary Orbit

Global reach, orbital momentum, and interconnected digital ecosystems.

03. Quantum Circuit

Nodes, circuit traces, and qubit terminals forming the agile Q tail.

Primary Horizontal Lockup

Clear Space & Safe Size Rule

To ensure optimal legibility and brand impact, the logo must always maintain an exclusion zone defined by $X$ (proportional to the diameter of the central Phi node) on all four sides. No text or graphic elements may enter this boundary.
