Logo Misuse & Rules

To maintain brand consistency and authority, strictly follow these usage guidelines.

Correct
Maintain Aspect Ratio & Clear Space

Always preserve official proportions with minimum clear space of 1X height around the mark.

Don't
Do Not Stretch or Distort

Never scale horizontally or vertically disproportionately or alter typography kerning.

Don't
Do Not Rotate or Tilt

The logo lockup must always be presented horizontally in standard orientation.

Don't
Do Not Use Unapproved Colors

Never apply unauthorized colors or place the mark on low-contrast backgrounds.
