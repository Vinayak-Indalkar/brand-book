BRAND COLOR 13

Color Usage

To maintain visual balance and brand consistency across all touchpoints, adhere to our 50/30/20 color distribution ratio. Utilize white as your dominant canvas to create ample breathing room. Apply black strategically for typography and structural contrast, and reserve our signature blue gradient as an impactful accent for key features and calls to action.

TYPOGRAPHY 14

Typography

PRIMARY TYPEFACE
Aa Lexend

Lexend is a sans-serif typeface engineered to improve reading proficiency. It features expanded character spacing and unique letterforms to reduce visual stress, offering a modern, highly accessible aesthetic.

SECONDARY TYPEFACE
Aa Noto Sans

Noto is a global font collection for writing in all modern and ancient languages. Noto Sans is an unmodulated (“sans serif”) design for texts in the Latin, Cyrillic and Greek scripts, which is also suitable as the complementary choice for other script-specific Noto Sans fonts. It has italic styles, multiple weights and widths, and 3,741 glyphs.

PATTERN 17

Pattern Usage

This geometric pattern reflects the structural integrity of blockchain networks and quantum data. Use it as a subtle background texture to add visual interest and reinforce our technological identity. Ensure the pattern always maintains a low contrast against the background color so that foreground text and imagery remain fully legible and prominent.
